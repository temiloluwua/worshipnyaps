Child Safety Standards

Our standards and practices for preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on Yaps.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Our commitment

Yaps (operated by Worship N Yaps) has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Content or conduct that sexualizes, endangers, or exploits minors is strictly prohibited and will be removed. We cooperate fully with law enforcement and report violations to the appropriate authorities.

What is prohibited

  • Any child sexual abuse material (CSAM), or links to it.
  • Sexualization of minors, including in text, images, or discussion.
  • Grooming, enticement, sextortion, or any attempt to sexually exploit a minor.
  • Trafficking, solicitation, or endangerment of children.
  • Sharing a minor's personal or contact information for harmful purposes.

How we prevent and respond to CSAE

  • Age gating: users confirm they meet our minimum age, and under-18 users receive a restricted, read-only experience.
  • In-app reporting: every message, post, comment, and profile can be reported directly in the app.
  • Blocking: users can block others; blocking immediately hides that user's content and messages.
  • Content filtering: user content is screened on submission, and prohibited content is removed.
  • Action on violations: we remove violating content, ban offending accounts, preserve relevant evidence, and report CSAE to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or local law enforcement.

Report a concern

To report suspected CSAE or child endangerment on Yaps, email us immediately with any usernames, screenshots, or details:

Email worshipnyaps@gmail.com

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services (911 in the US/Canada). You can also report directly to the NCMEC CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org.

Our compliance

These standards align with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and applicable laws regarding the reporting of CSAE. We review and update them as our product and legal obligations evolve.
