Delete Your Account

Request permanent deletion of your Worship & Yapps account and the personal data associated with it.

In the app

The fastest way is directly in the app — this removes your account and associated personal data immediately:

Open delete in the app

Or do it manually: tap your avatar (top-right) → Settings → Delete Account → confirm. You'll be asked to sign in first if you aren't already.

By email

Prefer us to handle it? Send a request and we'll take care of it:

Request account deletion

Send it from the email address on your account so we can verify it's you. We complete verified requests within 30 days and email you to confirm.

What gets deleted

DeletedKept (and why)
Your profile (name, username, bio, avatar), email and phone, your topics, community posts, comments, direct & group messages, event RSVPs, connections, and notifications. Records we're legally required to retain (e.g. transaction/receipt records for purchases) are kept only as long as the law requires, then deleted. Anonymized, non-identifying data may remain.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Note: deleting your account cannot be reversed. If you only want to stop notifications or take a break, you can sign out instead — no need to delete.
